What is exact here?

The solver creates a bipartite graph from Cube A voxels to Cube B voxels. Each edge represents assigning one source voxel to one of eight rigidly transformed pieces. A min-cost perfect matching uses every source and destination voxel exactly once. Therefore every displayed candidate has zero overlap, zero void, and zero out-of-bounds material. That is exact occupancy, not automatically a usable puzzle. Structural admissibility additionally requires connected, adequately sized, non-speckled pieces with bounded interface roughness.
