One manufactured kit of seven unique parts. The kit packs the cube in seven ways; five of those are painted as outer-skin sections (#3 and #6 omitted so the split stays readable). A section is painted iff that same local face sits on the outer 3×3×3 skin in that layout — including faces buried in the current packing. Unpainted area keeps the piece color (use Gray piece colors to neutralize it). Explode keeps the marks on each part.

    Export

    OBJ uses the same Y-up frame as the browser and downloads a matching .mtl with the family colors (keep both files together). In Rhino leave “Map OBJ Y to Rhino Z” on, and turn on “Set display color from OBJ material color.” If the front corner is yawed, select all and Rotate 90° (or 180°) around World Z. STL has no color or object groups.
